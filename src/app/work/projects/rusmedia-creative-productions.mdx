## Overview

RUSMEDIA serves as the premier multimedia and visual production unit for institutional events, formal symposiums, and cultural festivals at SMK Raden Umar Said. This initiative encompasses dynamic broadcast graphics, synchronized live video capture, and polished post-production storytelling.

## Production Highlights

- **Visual Identity & Broadcast Overlays**: Designed coherent motion graphic packages, lower thirds, countdown stingers, and title cards configured for real-time OBS and vMix live workflows.
- **Multi-Angle Camera Operations**: Managed professional mirrorless rigs, prime lenses, and audio gear during high-tempo live events to capture crisp documentary footage.
- **Post-Production Pipeline**: Executed color grading, sound design balancing, and paced editorial cuts across highlight reels and promotional teasers under strict turnaround windows.
- **Team Coordination**: Led technical setups during event rehearsals, establishing signal chains and communication protocols between audio engineers, camera operators, and stage directors.

## Tools & Gear

- **Hardware**: Sony Alpha Series, DJI Gimbals, Wireless Lavalier Audio Systems
- **Creative Suite**: Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Photoshop & Illustrator

## Outcome

Produced engaging visual collateral that reached thousands of viewers across social platforms and live audiences, elevating the professional presentation of institutional ceremonies.
